## Relic drop odds

These are the baseline chances that a kill drops a relic of any tier. The game interpolates between the three floor anchors. Floor 1 and Floor 11 are reference points for the Biome Boss curve, since Biome Bosses are on every fifth floor.

| Enemy role | Floor 1 anchor | Floor 11 anchor | Floor 50 anchor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Regular | 4% | 6.5% | 8.5% |
| Elite | 16% | 24% | 34% |
| Floor Alpha | 32% | 40% | 45% |
| Biome Boss | 38% | 44% | 49% |

Regular enemies normally roll Tier 1–2. Elites can also roll Tier 3. Floor Alphas and Biome Bosses normally roll Tier 1, 2, or 4. A qualifying first clear forces the killer's relic drop, and a first Biome Boss clear upgrades a Tier 1 roll to Tier 2. Affixes and tower events can change these odds.
